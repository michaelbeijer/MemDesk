Your board, your notes and your week, inside Gmail.

Every card is an email. Every note is a message in your own mailbox. Your calendar, with your tasks in it. No server, no database, nothing to sign up for.

Get MemDesk See it on GitHub

Chrome on your computer · the Gmail app and a home‑screen app on Android

Board

A board made of your email

Columns are Gmail labels and cards are threads. Drag a card and its label moves with it; drop it on Done and it leaves the Inbox.

Give a card your own title, a note and a colour without touching the email itself. And while you read an email, one button at the bottom of Gmail files it in a column.

Notes

Notes that live in Gmail

Headings, bold and italics, lists, checklists you tick, links, and tables you can sort. Notes save as you type, and a paste from Word, Excel, Google Docs, a web page or Markdown arrives formatted.

Folders nest as deep as you like. The Scratchpad is always open: one note for whatever needs writing down right now, the same on every computer and phone.

Calendar

Your week, tasks and all

Your Google Calendar with Google Tasks woven into it: the week, the month, or the next four weeks as one list.

A task with a date sits in its day, ready to tick. The ones without a date, and any that are overdue, wait at the side. A task made from an email opens the email.

Phone

And on your phone

A home-screen app with the board, the notes and the calendar. The notes open straight onto your Scratchpad, ready to type, with the same editor, folders and search as in Chrome.

And a panel in the Gmail app puts the open email on the board: one tap for its column.

Yours alone

The board and the notes are views of your own mailbox, and the calendar of your own Google Calendar and Tasks. Nothing passes through anyone else.

No MemDesk serverIt talks only to Google, from your own browser or your own Apps Script.
Nothing to sign up forNo account, no database, no analytics, no tracking.
Never sends mailAnd never deletes anything for good: only its own notes go to Trash.
Calendar read-onlyIt reads your events and tasks to show them, and cannot change them.
